Flat UI restyles the default interface of WoW: Forever with flat backgrounds and softly shaded bar fills, borderless by default or with thin dark borders. It has its own options window and profiles, and handles to move and size frames and windows. It draws combat numbers in front of enemy nameplates and in scroll areas beside your character, and in your bags and bank it marks items that bind when equipped, tints items you cannot use red and marks the food a hunter's pet eats.

![Flat UI in a fight: the player and target frames, the enemy's cast bar, combat numbers in front of the nameplate, proc art beside the character and the action bars](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dismlx/flat-ui/main/media/flatui-in-play.jpg)

Every setting starts at the settings of **Jon Default**, the author's own profile, and a new install starts with its frame positions, fitted to your screen. Type `/flatui` in game to open the options window, or `/flatui setup` for a short walk round your screen.
